## 4. Pre-Purchase Verification Checklist

Before releasing any pilot funds, the importer must complete the mandatory 11-point verification gate:
1. Verify operating fleet density of Seagull, bZ4X, and ID.4 in Addis Ababa.
2. Interview at least 5 Addis Ababa EV garages and 2 fleet operators on actual turnover.
3. Obtain recent repair orders confirming real replacement frequency.
4. Verify exact OEM part numbers against local vehicle VINs (especially Chinese domestic FAW/SAIC variants).
5. Obtain written quotations from at least two independent suppliers per SKU.
6. Confirm stock availability, minimum order quantities (MOQs), and lead times.
7. Confirm packaged shipping weight and dimensions for freight calculations.
8. Calculate full landed cost (FOB + sea/air freight + insurance + 15% VAT + customs tariff).
9. Audit local competitor availability and pricing in Bole, Piazza, and Kera spare-parts corridors.
10. Confirm supplier return, warranty, and defective-item handling policies.
11. Stress-test total capital requirements against the $5,000–$10,000 budget cap.
