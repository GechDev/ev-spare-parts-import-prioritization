The Importer's Dilemma: Capital vs. Stockouts

Ethiopian EV Macro Context (2024–2026)

  • Regulatory Shock: 2024 ICE import ban catalysed massive EV adoption across Addis Ababa.
  • Support Deficit: Official OEM dealer parts networks are virtually non-existent; aftermarket is heavily fragmented.
  • Capital Constraint: Initial pilot capital of $5,000–$10,000 severely penalizes dead inventory.
  • The Dilemma: Bulky sheet metal traps cash; stockouts of routine maintenance stall operating fleets.

Minute 3:15 – 4:30 | Scoring Methodology

Explainable Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)

Approved Baseline Scoring Formula

Score = 0.25·F1 + 0.25·F2 + 0.20·F3 + 0.20·F4 + 0.10·F5

  • F1 (25%): Demand Evidence (Maintenance intervals & defect signals).
  • F2 (25%): International Procurement Delay (Supplier lead times & stockout risk).
  • F3 (20%): Vehicle Compatibility (Platform interchangeability & trim spread).
  • F4 (20%): Sourcing Practicality (Acquisition unit cost & freight density).
  • F5 (10%): Evidence Confidence (Multi-source catalog verification).

Economic Logic

Aligns with ABC-XYZ inventory classification: High-velocity, low-cost consumables (X/C) protect working capital. Bulky, high-cost body panels (Z/A) tie up foreign exchange and suffer high holding penalties.

Minute 5:30 – 6:30 | Integrity & Governance

Evidence Limitations & Materiality Hierarchy

Disclosed Materiality Hierarchy

  • Rank 1 (Severe): No Ethiopian vehicle registration census data.
  • Rank 2 (Severe): Landed cost gap (Djibouti port clearance & Ethiopian tariffs).
  • Rank 3 (High): Single-supplier price points on certain SKUs.
  • Rank 4 (Medium): NHTSA geographic mismatch (US focus vs Chinese domestic).
  • Rank 5 (Medium): Year and trim fitment variances across production years.

Governance Disclaimer

PROVISIONAL RECOMMENDATION

The five candidates should be treated as provisional import-prioritization recommendations, not confirmed purchase orders, unless the evidence establishes exact fitment, local demand, supplier reliability, and landed costs.
