## 2. Selection Methodology & Decision Framework

Rather than relying purely on classified listing counts—which measure seller marketing activity rather than actual vehicle fleet size—the selection methodology evaluates candidates across four objective, balanced decision criteria:

```
                  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │ 1. PORTFOLIO MARKET SEGMENT DIVERSITY        │
                  │    (Mass urban, executive, family crossover)│
                  └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                         │
                  ┌──────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                  │ 2. CATALOG OPENNESS & SUPPLY CHAIN ACCESS    │
                  │    (Traceable OEM part numbers & pricing)    │
                  └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                         │
                  ┌──────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                  │ 3. MODULAR PLATFORM INTERCHANGEABILITY       │
                  │    (Component sharing across trims/models)   │
                  └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                         │
                  ┌──────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                  │ 4. DEFECT BASELINES & WEAR PREDICTABILITY    │
                  │    (NHTSA recalls, maintenance schedules)    │
                  └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Portfolio Market Segment Diversity:**  
   An early-stage importer with finite capital must avoid concentration risk. Stocking parts for only one vehicle class creates vulnerability if that segment's adoption stalls. The selected trio creates a balanced, resilient portfolio:
   * *Entry-Level Urban Commuter:* High volume, price-sensitive private buyers and ride-hailers (**BYD Seagull**).
   * *Executive / Corporate Crossover:* Premium corporate, diplomatic, and NGO buyers with high willingness to pay for downtime prevention (**Toyota bZ4X**).
   * *Upper-Middle Family Crossover:* Widespread parallel-import family vehicle with high utility (**Volkswagen ID.4**).
2. **Catalog Openness & Supply Chain Traceability:**  
   Selected models must have public, structured online parts documentation with verifiable OEM part numbers and pricing across our primary catalog source pack (`Landin Parts`, `CQSW Auto Parts`, `Toyota Parts`, `Exist.ae`).
3. **Modular Platform Component Interchangeability:**  
   Models built on shared modular architectures (BYD e-Platform 3.0, Toyota e-TNGA, VW MEB) allow imported parts to service multiple vehicle models or trims, protecting against inventory obsolescence.
4. **Empirical Defect Signal Availability:**  
   Availability of technical service bulletins, recall histories, or standardized maintenance intervals to substantiate wear velocity without fabricating numbers.

## 5. Comparative Defense: Why Selected vs. Why Eliminated

### Defense A: Why BYD Seagull was selected over Changan Benben E-Star
* **The Evaluator's Challenge:** *"Changan Benben was imported earlier (2021–2022) than the Seagull (2023–2024), so Benben vehicles are older and need replacement parts right now. Why didn't you pick Changan?"*
* **The Defensible Answer:**  
  1. **Supply Chain Traceability:** Catalog documentation for Changan Benben in open, English-language international databases is highly fragmented. OEM part numbers are difficult to cross-verify online without proprietary Chinese dealer software, creating severe procurement risks for an independent importer. In contrast, suppliers like `CQSW` have published dedicated, open part-number indexes specifically for the BYD Seagull.
  2. **Market Momentum & Dealer Stocking:** Adey Market records nearly triple the visibility for the Seagull (806 listings) compared to the Benben (282 listings), and Gezat Electric actively showcases the Seagull in its primary catalog.
  3. **Platform Longevity:** The Seagull is built on BYD's flagship e-Platform 3.0, ensuring long-term parts availability across the broader BYD Ocean series, whereas the Benben is built on an older, electrified ICE chassis.

### Defense B: Why BYD Seagull was selected over BYD Yuan Up
* **The Evaluator's Challenge:** *"Adey Market shows 1,370 listings for the Yuan Up, compared to only 806 for the Seagull. If you follow the data, shouldn't you pick the vehicle with the most listings?"*
* **The Defensible Answer:**  
  1. **The Proxy Boundary Rule:** Slide 4 and Slide 6 notes explicitly state that marketplace listings are proxies for seller activity and distributor stocking attempts, **not vehicle registrations or sales**. A high listing count often reflects dealer speculation or parallel importers marketing new inventory.
  2. **Lifecycle & Wear Velocity:** The Yuan Up was released globally in early 2024. Vehicles on the road in Ethiopia are brand new; owners will not require replacement brake discs, shock absorbers, or suspension arms for at least 12 to 24 months. In contrast, the Seagull has been in circulation longer, accumulates intense urban stop-and-go mileage, and has faster component turnover.
  3. **Capital Risk:** Yuan Up parts carry higher unit purchase costs than Seagull parts, increasing the capital required for an initial pilot import.

### Defense C: Why Toyota bZ4X was selected
* **The Evaluator's Challenge:** *"The bZ4X is an expensive vehicle with lower unit volume than budget Chinese EVs. Why include it?"*
* **The Defensible Answer:**  
  1. **Brand Dominance & Willingness to Pay:** Toyota is the foundational automotive brand in Ethiopia. Toyota EV owners (predominantly corporate fleets, executives, and international organizations) exhibit the highest willingness to pay for genuine maintenance parts to eliminate vehicle downtime.
  2. **Catalog Precision & Engineering Evidence:** Toyota's global parts catalog system (`toyota-spare-parts.com`) is the most structured in the world, with standardized 10-digit part numbering and complete US NHTSA safety recall/defect documentation. Including the bZ4X anchors our dataset in verifiable, high-confidence evidence.

### Defense D: Why Volkswagen ID.4 was selected over Volkswagen ID.6
* **The Evaluator's Challenge:** *"Why did you select the ID.4 rather than the larger ID.6?"*
* **The Defensible Answer:**  
  1. **Market Visibility Ratio:** On Adey Market, the ID.4 family accounts for **575 listings** (471 FAW-VW ID.4 Crozz + 104 General ID.4), compared to only **145 listings** for the ID.6 (a 4:1 visibility ratio).
  2. **Middle East Supply Corridor:** ID.4 parts have dedicated catalog coverage on `Exist.ae` in Dubai, enabling short sea freight transit (1–2 weeks) to the Port of Djibouti.
  3. **Platform Subsumption:** Because the ID.4 and ID.6 share the modular MEB platform, prioritizing the ID.4 captures the high-turnover braking, suspension, and filtration components that also fit the ID.6, without tying up capital in bulky, ID.6-exclusive 3-row body panels.

## 6. Selection Limitations & Defensible Boundary Statements

To maintain absolute academic and professional integrity, the following limitations are explicitly documented as part of our defense:

1. **Marketplace Listings are Visibility Proxies Only:**  
   The listing figures recorded (e.g., 1,370 for Yuan Up, 1,042 for bZ series, 806 for Seagull, 575 for ID.4, 282 for Benben) reflect seller classified ads aggregated across Telegram channels and digital dealerships. They **do not represent vehicle registrations, official sales volumes, or circulating fleet size**. No claims of national market share or vehicle population are made without official governmental supporting evidence.
2. **Absence of Official Registration Statistics:**  
   Neither the Ethiopian Ministry of Transport and Logistics nor the Addis Ababa City Driver and Vehicle Licensing Authority publishes open-access, model-disaggregated electric vehicle registration counts. Selecting vehicles based on commercial visibility and distributor activity is the most defensible proxy available.
3. **Geographic Asymmetry in Defect Data:**  
   NHTSA safety and recall data applies to the Toyota bZ4X and Volkswagen ID.4 as global platforms, providing valuable engineering failure baselines. However, Chinese domestic models (BYD Seagull) are unlisted in NHTSA databases. This gap is recorded as a structural catalog limitation rather than filled with fabricated numbers.
4. **Provisional Recommendation Status:**  
   The vehicle selection and subsequent parts prioritization remain strictly **provisional**. They are designed to guide a low-risk pilot procurement tranche ($5,000–$10,000) rather than a full commercial container commitment.
